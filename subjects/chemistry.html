✳03 / TRANSFORM

SUBJECT NOTES

Chemistry.

Chemistry is a closer look at what things are made of, how their particles behave, and how substances can change.

2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O

01 / THE SPARK

Why I like this subject

I like that chemistry explains changes I can see—such as colour changes, bubbles, or a new substance forming—using ideas about atoms and reactions that are too small to see directly.

It feels like solving a mystery by connecting evidence to what might be happening at particle level.

02 / WHAT I’M LEARNING

Which chapters that I study now in this subject do I like the most

I enjoy learning about chemical reactions and equations, acids and bases, metals and non-metals, and carbon compounds when they come up in our current syllabus.

Balancing equations is satisfying because it shows that matter is conserved even as substances rearrange.

03 / WHAT IT GIVES ME

What were the fruits of this subject in my life

Chemistry has helped me pay closer attention to materials and changes around me. I’m learning to look for evidence, describe it clearly, and use a model to explain what I observe.

It has also shown me how much of everyday life—from food to fuels—depends on chemistry.
