Maths.

Maths gives me a language for the patterns underneath things: structure, motion, scale, and the graphs that connect an equation to the world.

01 / THE SPARK

Why I like this subject

To me, maths is the foundation of the universe around us. Patterns, quantities, shapes, and relationships help describe everything from a planet’s path through space to the structure of matter.

I love looking for the mathematics hiding in the real world—and finding that a few clear ideas can explain something vast.

02 / WHAT I’M LEARNING

Which chapters that I study now in this subject do I like the most

In Grade 10 algebra, I enjoy Polynomials, Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables, Quadratic Equations, and Arithmetic Progressions.

In trigonometry, I like Introduction to Trigonometry, Trigonometric Identities, and Heights and Distances. Graphs are a favourite because they turn equations into something I can see and compare.

Chapter titles follow the CBSE Class X Mathematics syllabus for 2026–27.
