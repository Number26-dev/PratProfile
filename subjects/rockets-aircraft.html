SUBJECT NOTES

Rockets &
aircraft.

I’m drawn to the engineering that lets an aircraft cut through the sky or a rocket push beyond it. Flight turns forces, materials, and bold ideas into motion.

F = ma  ·  action ↔ reaction

01 / THE SPARK

Why I like this subject

Rockets and aircraft bring science and engineering together in a way I can picture. Shape, mass, thrust, drag, and control all matter; one design choice can change an entire flight.

I like tracing those forces and imagining the cycle of testing, learning, and redesign that makes each machine better.

02 / WHAT I’M LEARNING

Which chapters that I study now in this subject do I like the most

Rockets and aircraft aren’t usually a single school chapter, so I connect them to the topics I’m learning in science and maths: forces and motion, Newton’s laws, pressure, graphs, and geometry.

I especially like seeing those ideas turn into lift, thrust, trajectories, and a real path through the air.

03 / WHAT IT GIVES ME

What were the fruits of this subject in my life

This interest gives me questions to explore and motivates me to learn more physics, maths, and engineering. It has also taught me that ambitious designs begin with understanding basic principles.

It keeps space exploration and aviation on my list of things I hope to understand more deeply.
